# RIFTWATCH asset credits

- **Old Quarter** is adapted from [Urban / City street](https://blendswap.com/blend/24622) by [Evolix777](https://blendswap.com/profile/1123479), licensed [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). RIFTWATCH converted the Blender scene to glTF, adapted materials, added traversable ground and boundary cover, and scaled it for player characters.
- **Island low-poly tree** is adapted from [Low-Poly Tree](https://blendswap.com/blend/5671) by [laser blaster](https://blendswap.com/blend/5671), licensed [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). RIFTWATCH selected the lighter tree mesh, adapted bark and leaf materials, and converted it to glTF.

The downloaded Blender source files are not included in the game. The optimized game assets are `models/web/old_quarter.glb` and `models/web/blendswap_lowpoly_tree.glb`.

## Combat audio (2026-10-17)
- Rifle recordings: Tabasco, Gunshot Sounds, https://opengameart.org/content/gunshot-sounds — CC0. Three SKS shots cropped from sks.wav, converted to mono, normalized and faded; used as rifle effects rather than an exact M4 recording. Files: public/assets/audio/rifle-1.wav through rifle-3.wav.
- Punch impacts: Kenney, Impact Sounds, https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds — CC0. impactPunch_heavy_000 through 002, renamed punch-1.ogg through punch-3.ogg.
